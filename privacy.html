Privacy Policy

Kings & Chaos · Effective 29 September 2026

Kings & Chaos is made by Killer Potatos Worldwide, LLC ("we"). This page says what the game collects, why, who else handles it, and how you can delete it. Questions: killerpotatos.ww@gmail.com.

Playing without an account

Games against the computer and Pass & Play work without an account. Your settings, records and saved game stay on your device and are never sent to us.

What we collect when you play online

  • Your account: the username you pick, a random ID for your device, and how you chose to sign in again: an email address and password (the password is stored hashed, never as plain text), or an ID from Google or Apple. From Google and Apple we receive only an ID that identifies your account to us; we don't ask them for your name or email.
  • Your games: matches, moves, results and your rating, so games can be played over days, checked for fair play, and shown on leaderboards.
  • Chat: messages you send in a match's chat, shown to the other player. Words on our filter list are starred out.
  • Reports and blocks: if you report a player, we keep the report and the last messages of that match's chat so we can review it. Players you block are kept on your account.
  • Notifications: if you turn on "your turn" notifications, a notification token for your browser or device.
  • Technical logs: our servers keep short-lived logs (such as IP addresses and errors) to keep the game running and secure.

We don't sell your information, and we don't use it for advertising. The game has no ads or analytics trackers today; if that changes, we'll update this page first.

Who else handles it

  • DigitalOcean hosts our game server and its database (United States).
  • GitHub Pages hosts the web version of the game.
  • Google Firebase delivers notifications, if you turn them on.
  • Google and Apple handle signing in, if you choose them. Their own privacy policies apply to what you do on their sign-in pages.

How long we keep it

Until you delete your account. Reports about a player (with the match and its chat's last messages) and problem reports you send us from Settings are kept for moderation and support, and emailed to our support address. Nightly backups are kept for 14 days and weekly server backups for 4 weeks, so deleted data is gone from backups within about 5 weeks.

Deleting your account

In the game: Settings → Online account → Delete. This ends your matches (your opponent wins), removes your username from them, deletes your chat messages, and then deletes your account and everything stored with it. You can also email us to ask for deletion, or to ask what we hold about you.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or move your data, and to object to how it's used. Email us and we'll answer within 30 days.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has made an account, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and its date. Big changes will also be shown in the game.
